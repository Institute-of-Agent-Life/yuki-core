# Bind group memory to the person the original speaker actually mentioned

Normal: A speaks in a group and explicitly records a claim about the one original mentioned Person B. Store B as the group-scoped subject and A as the author of the exact quote. A later explicit correction of attribution versions A’s previously read group-scoped fact: retain the superseded original, keep its author and the group boundary, and create a separate third-party fact about B. A new or unproved third party must never gain authority over the original.

Faults: private input, ambiguous or unassigned mentions, self/bot or unobserved aliases, cross-group reads, stale revisions, an unverifiable quote, a missing old read or a partial transfer leave both subjects unchanged. Source erasure scrubs copied content and citations. A cold duplicate returns only its first receipt. Capture the original transitions and copied evidence before implementing the paid operation.
